# 2026-10-27 OWNER PRIORITY: IDEA FIRST, 60–70% DEMONSTRABLE PRODUCT FIDELITY, >=80% BEST
This is a strategic design decision approved in chat by owner. It supersedes older assumptions that narrow one-operation pilot is the entire intended product. It does **NOT** turn a target completion percentage into measured progress, alter contest organizer rules, certify integration/paid market transaction, or authorize secret sharing/source publication. Do not treat "implementation 70%" as prize probability.

## Owner's principle and precedence
1. **IDEA FIRST**: clearly articulate coherent product concept and real sponsor/user value, not a miscellaneous feature list. Optimize proposal/pitch/judge demo for tangible product differentiation.
2. **IMPLEMENTED FIDELITY**: 60–70% of the **approved full-product vision** working at a smaller scale is OWNER SUCCESS target; >=80% is BEST outcome. The remainder can be developed by owner or sponsor after the event. Global market rank/top 2/top 3 is aspirational only.
3. A 60–70% claim is only legitimate for **demonstrated behavior** assessed by a fixed scorecard; design-only, screenshots, synthetic offline tests, mocks, narrative or prospective connector logos do **not** count as real integration. Show "design coverage" separately from "working implementation coverage" and never combine denominators.
4. Official contest eligibility, permissions, originality, IP/licensing, payment authorization, actual provider E2E, exact final deliverable and receipt are independent **hard gates** regardless of estimated %. No bypassing them for idea-first.
5. Limit implementation to one coherent end-to-end demo of the idea; connect all essential components at small scale where technically lawful, rather than promise full global scale. No new repo/projects/purchases, no external data transfer, unpaid APIs or source visibility changes without explicit owner approval. Expanded roadmap does not change KIC final frozen scope.

## PayPal measurable vision rubric — 100 candidate acceptance points (proposed scorecard)
A. AI job-to-freelancer marketplace journey — **25**: user posts realistic requirement, deterministic eligibility + authorized Gemini explanation, user sees matching/quote and can choose; clearly label synthetic profiles, no fake global network.
B. PayPal transaction rail — **25**: genuine explicitly approved Sandbox buyer->capture->provider GET end-to-end, persisted merchant/amount/session ownership, idempotency and unknown handling. Synthetic/fake provider cannot score this category.
C. SOVIA AI action audit/safety — **20**: user-observable agent proposal, human approval and payment provider truth with evidence and actual fail-closed findings. Full standalone Python SOVIA runtime integration only earns its separately defined advanced acceptance; don't claim a wrapper is SOVIA service.
D. DBVORA data-management visibility — **15**: tested read-only schema/health/persistence consistency and distinct DBVORA adapter verified vs DBVORA-inspired small node:sqlite panel clearly labeled. No raw personal or secret leakage, no destructive archiving demo.
E. PayPal decision intelligence — **15**: working privacy-minimized event-derived funnel/demand/exception metrics with clear source, time period, denominators, synthetic-vs-real flag and one evidenced sponsor decision hypothesis. No actual customer population demand claim.
**Each category is graded by verified acceptance subtests with source + runnable demo + judge-visible output.** Total is proposed evaluation instrumentation, not pre-claimed implementation. Owner's 60–70%=success and >=80%=best after eligibility; 100 means comprehensive vision, not mass real-user scale. A working Sandbox remains mandatory independently of scores.

The contest ZIP stays markdown-only SKILL.md/references; never include Python/JS scripts or n8n runtime inside it.

## Demonstration architecture choices
- PayPal preferred: one judge-runnable Node.js AI->authorized PayPal Sandbox flow plus **actual minimal SOVIA-style audit**, **actual minimal DBVORA-style database read-only evidence** and **actual small privacy-safe analytics** using current ledger, while keeping adapters to existing SOVIA/DBVORA products explicit and honestly deferred if not fully integrated. Distinguish components by names in UI and docs; avoid IP copying from private sources into public code. Working feature parity beats speculative servers.
- UGREEN preferred: bounded Uliya Skill uses natural-language slot filling to assemble a multi-step plan from installed official Expert Agents, then executes/checks only authorized supported operations; broader n8n-compatible ecosystem is product vision/extension, not an illegal executable in submitted ZIP. If official chaining is unsupported, no fake path; explain the tested narrower effect and show future design to judges as roadmap.
- The 60–70/80 scorecard does not give permission to unilaterally skip owner-identified PayPal DBVORA/SOVIA/BI roles or UGREEN workflow composer. Owners retain full vision. If hard contest/deadline makes a category impossible, note tradeoff and continue highest-value eligible core.
